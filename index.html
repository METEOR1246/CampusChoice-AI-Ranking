CampusChoice

A transparent, rule-based ranking of university events for the demo user.

Demonstration data: every event below is fictional and was created for this assignment prototype. The events are not real University of Birmingham activities and no bookings can be made.

Ranked events

Ordered by score (highest first). Equal scores are broken by event ID.

    Scoring: Score = 0.5 × Interest + 0.3 × Time + 0.2 × Affordability. This is a transparent rule-based ranking, not a trained AI model.
